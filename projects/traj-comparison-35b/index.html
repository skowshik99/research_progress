New generator scripts/agents/pope_datagen_clean/datagen/gen_traj_comparison.py reviews the FAILED trajectory one step at a time, showing the judge the full SUCCESSFUL reference and the full FAILED trajectory plus a callout of the step under review. For every step judged wrong it emits a correction record: the failed agent's own decision context, the wrong action it took, a better bash command, and the reasoning. This is LLM-only (no container replay), so the proposed better command is not executed/verified.
